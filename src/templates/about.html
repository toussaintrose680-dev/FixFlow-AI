About FixFlow-AI

AI-powered IT support that helps you find the problem and get back on track.

FixFlow-AI is an AI-powered IT support platform designed to help people troubleshoot technology problems in a simple and accessible way.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make IT support easier to access by using artificial intelligence to help users understand technology problems and identify possible solutions.

Created by Roselene Toussaint

FixFlow-AI was created by Roselene Toussaint as a technology project focused on making IT troubleshooting more accessible through artificial intelligence.

The goal is to continue developing FixFlow-AI into a useful platform that can help people solve everyday technology issues.
